# Uploading the demo to YouTube

Files in this folder:
- `openpath-demo.mp4`: the video (2:57, 1280×720, with voiceover)
- `youtube-thumbnail.png`: the custom thumbnail (1280×720)
- `openpath-demo.en.srt`: English subtitles, timed to the voiceover

## Title

```
Stop Invoice Fraud Before Your AI Agent Pays | Openpath
```

## Description

```
AI agents are starting to pay invoices, and an AI can be talked into a yes. Business email compromise, the "we changed banks" invoice scam, cost businesses about $2.77 billion in 2024 according to the FBI's Internet Crime Report.

Openpath sits between the AI agent and the money:
• Rules the AI cannot override: spending limits, known POs, approved vendors, and each vendor's bank account on file
```

## Steps

1. Go to https://studio.youtube.com and click **Create → Upload videos**.
2. Choose `openpath-demo.mp4`.
3. Paste the title and description above.
4. Under **Thumbnail**, upload `youtube-thumbnail.png`.
5. Under **Show more → Tags**, paste the tags.
6. Choose **No, it's not made for kids**.
7. On the **Video elements** step, skip ahead. On **Visibility**, choose **Public** (or **Unlisted** if you only want to share the link).
8. After publishing, open **Subtitles → Add language → English → Upload file → With timing**, and choose `openpath-demo.en.srt`.
9. Copy the video link for the submission form and your X post.

The chapters appear automatically because the description lists them starting at 0:00.
